# Revenue model

## What you sell (initial positioning)

B2B **AI Automation Services** for SMBs (5–200 employees):

- AI workflow automation
- AI call centers (inbound/outbound)
- AI chatbots (web + SMS)
- Lead generation systems
- End-to-end business automation

## Pricing tiers (seed playbooks)

| Tier | Price | Target buyer |
|------|-------|--------------|
| Starter pilot | $2,500 project | Ops manager testing ROI |
| Growth | $3,500–5,000/mo | 20–50 employee SMB |
| Scale | $7,500–12,000/mo | Multi-location or 50–200 employees |

## Unit economics (target)

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| CAC (AI-driven) | <$200 |
| ACV | $30,000–60,000/year |
| Gross margin | 70%+ (services + software) |
| Payback | <3 months |

## Revenue streams

1. **Implementation projects** — one-time automation builds  
2. **Monthly retainers** — managed AI agents + support  
3. **Usage-based voice** — per-minute overage on call centers  
4. **White-label** — agencies resell your stack (Phase 4)

## GTM motion (this system)

1. AI discovers 100 SMB leads/week in target verticals  
2. Score ≥60 → personalized email sequence  
3. Conversation AI books discovery calls  
4. Human closes deals >$10k; AI closes <$5k pilots via Stripe  
5. Onboarding → case study → referral upsell

## Expansion revenue

- Add channels (SMS, voice) after email proof  
- Upsell from chatbot → full automation suite  
- Cross-sell lead gen system to existing automation clients
